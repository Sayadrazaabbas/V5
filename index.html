02ABOUT CBGIPL

ONE PLATFORM. TWO ENGINES OF GROWTH.

Development expertise. Global commercial reach.

Carter Brown Global Infra Private Limited (CBGIPL) is a Mumbai-headquartered private limited company incorporated in India in 2026. Its platform spans real estate development, infrastructure and EPC, and global commodity trading.

Our mission is to develop urban infrastructure and real estate assets, facilitate efficient trade in essential commodities, and build long-term stakeholder value through integrity, innovation and execution excellence.

The business combines project origination and delivery with commercial coordination across oil and gas, fertilizers and metals. Each opportunity is evaluated on its own technical, commercial and regulatory merits.

06 / OIL & GAS TRADING

Energy products. Disciplined transactions.

Commercial coordination across petroleum and gas products.

Our trading scope includes refined products such as diesel/gasoil and jet fuel, plus LPG and LNG. Crude and feedstock opportunities are considered only where seller capability, specifications and destination compliance permit.

Spot or term transactions and CIF, CFR or FOB delivery terms are agreed contract by contract. CBGIPL coordinates counterparties, documentation, inspection, shipping interfaces and transaction execution.

Transaction considerations

  • Product grade and specification
  • Seller capability and origin
  • Buyer and counterparty verification
  • Inspection and shipping terms
  • Banking and documentary conditions
