02ABOUT CARTER BROWN GLOBAL

A GLOBAL PERSPECTIVE. A DIVERSIFIED VISION.

Building connections across industries and borders.

Carter Brown Global Infra Pvt. Ltd. (CBGIPL) is an India-based company with interests spanning international trade, commodity sourcing, energy, industrial materials and infrastructure development.
